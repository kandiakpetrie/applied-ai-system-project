# Grooming Guide

Routine coat, nail, and dental care, and how often each is needed.

## Brushing

Short-haired pets need brushing about once a week. Long-haired pets need daily
or near-daily brushing to prevent mats, especially behind the ears, under the
legs, and along the tail.

Cats groom themselves but still need help during shedding season; brushing also
reduces hairballs.

Brushing takes 5 to 15 minutes depending on coat length.

## Bathing

Dogs generally need a bath every four to six weeks, or sooner if they get into
something. Bathing too often strips the coat of natural oils.

Cats rarely need baths at all and usually only for a specific reason such as a
soiled coat.

Do not bathe within 48 hours of applying a topical flea or tick treatment.

## Nails

Nail trims are needed roughly every three to four weeks. If nails click on hard
floors, they are too long.

Trim a small amount at a time and stop before the pink quick. Many pets accept
one or two nails per session better than a full trim in one sitting.

## Teeth

Daily tooth brushing is the ideal for both dogs and cats; two to three times a
week still helps meaningfully.

Dental chews support but do not replace brushing. Persistent bad breath is a
reason for a vet check, not a grooming fix.

## Ears and eyes

Check ears weekly for redness, odour, or discharge, especially in floppy-eared
dogs and after swimming.

Wipe eye discharge with a damp cloth. Any squinting or coloured discharge
should be seen by a vet.

## Scheduling notes

- Grooming is usually low priority day to day but should not slip for weeks.
- Monthly tasks such as nail trims work best on a fixed date each month.
- Group quiet tasks such as brushing and ear checks into one calm session.
