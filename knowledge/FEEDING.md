# Feeding Guide

General feeding guidance for household pets. Always defer to the feeding amount
printed on the food label and to your veterinarian's instructions.

## Dogs

Most adult dogs eat two measured meals per day, roughly 10-12 hours apart.
Split the daily portion in half rather than adding extra food.

Puppies under six months usually need three to four smaller meals per day
because they cannot store enough energy between meals.

Fresh water should be available at all times. Leave 30 minutes of rest before
and after a meal before vigorous exercise or a long walk.

## Cats

Adult cats do well on two to three small meals per day. Cats graze naturally,
so a scheduled feeding of the same measured portion works better than leaving a
full bowl out all day.

Kittens under six months need three to four meals per day.

Cats drink less water than dogs and benefit from wet food or a water fountain.
Sudden refusal to eat for more than 24 hours is a reason to call a vet.

## Feeding around medication

Some medications must be given with food and others on an empty stomach. When a
medication says "give with food", schedule the dose at the same time as a meal
rather than as a separate task.

If a pet must fast before a procedure, cancel the meals in that window instead
of marking them complete.

## Scheduling notes

- Keep meal times consistent from day to day; pets anticipate them.
- Space meals at least four hours apart.
- A feeding task takes about 5 minutes of owner time; preparing fresh or raw
  food takes closer to 15 minutes.
