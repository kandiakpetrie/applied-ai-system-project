# Medication Guide

Notes on giving routine, already-prescribed medication. Dosages and drug
choices always come from a veterinarian, never from a schedule app.

## Timing and consistency

Most daily medications work best at the same time every day. A once-daily dose
is easiest to remember when it is attached to an existing routine such as the
morning feeding.

Twice-daily doses should be about 12 hours apart, for example 07:00 and 19:00.

Medication tasks are short (about 2 minutes) but high priority: a missed dose
matters far more than a delayed grooming session.

## Missed doses

If a dose is missed and the next one is still several hours away, give it as
soon as it is noticed. If the next dose is close, skip the missed one and
continue as normal. Never give two doses at once to catch up.

Record missed doses so the pattern can be reported at the next vet visit.

## Heartworm and flea prevention

Heartworm prevention is usually a monthly dose given on the same calendar day
each month. Flea and tick prevention is also typically monthly.

These are recurring monthly tasks rather than daily ones, and they are still
high priority because a lapse restarts the protection window.

## Storage and handling

Keep medication in its original labelled container. Refrigerated liquids should
come out only for the dose itself.

Wash hands after handling any topical treatment, and keep treated pets from
grooming each other for the time stated on the package.

## Conflicts with other care

Do not schedule a bath within 48 hours of a topical flea treatment; it washes
the product off.

If two pets need medication at the same moment, stagger them a few minutes
apart so each dose is given and observed properly.
