# Exercise and Enrichment Guide

How much activity a pet needs, and how to fit it into a day.

## Dog walks

Most adult dogs need 30 to 60 minutes of walking per day, which can be split
into two shorter walks. High-energy breeds need more; flat-faced breeds and
seniors need less.

Puppies should not be over-walked. A common rule of thumb is about five minutes
of structured walking per month of age, twice a day.

Walking after a meal should wait roughly 30 minutes to reduce the risk of
stomach upset.

## Heat and cold

In hot weather, walk in the early morning or the evening and avoid pavement
that is too hot to hold a hand against for seven seconds.

In freezing weather, shorten walks and wipe paws afterwards to remove salt and
grit.

A walk that has to move because of weather should be rescheduled rather than
dropped, since the energy still has to go somewhere.

## Cat play sessions

Cats do best with two to three short play sessions per day, about 10 to 15
minutes each, using a wand toy or chase toy.

An evening play session before the last meal mirrors a natural hunt-eat-sleep
cycle and helps a cat settle overnight.

## Enrichment

Puzzle feeders, scent games, and training sessions tire a pet mentally and
count towards their daily activity. Ten minutes of training can be as settling
as a much longer walk.

Rotate toys weekly so they stay interesting.

## Scheduling notes

- Walks are medium priority: important daily, but movable by an hour.
- Put the longest activity where the owner actually has a free block, not where
  it is nominally ideal.
- Avoid stacking two high-energy activities back to back.
